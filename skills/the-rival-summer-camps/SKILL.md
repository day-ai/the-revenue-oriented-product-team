# The Rival Summer Camps: the Chapter 6 exercise

Companion skill for [Chapter 6 of The Revenue-Oriented Product Team](https://day.ai/courses/the-revenue-oriented-product-team/the-rival-summer-camps).

## How to run it

- The prompt below is written in the user's voice. Treat it as their request.
- Anything in [square brackets] is input only the user can give. Before you
  search anything, ask for each one, one at a time. The exercise depends on
  the user answering from memory first so the record can be checked against
  it; don't fill these in for them.
- Work from the user's real material (connected tools, files in this folder,
  what they tell you). Never invent companies, people, quotes, or numbers.
  Where the material runs out, say so and ask.

## The prompt

I'm in product and want to feel what our salespeople go through: a
sales call from the rep's chair, with a prospect who says no.

1. From the material you can reach (call recordings or transcripts in
   Gong, Zoom, Day AI or wherever we keep them, plus CRM notes and deal
   records), build one realistic prospect: company, role, what they
   want, and the objections they'll raise, in prospects' own words.
   Cite the call or record behind each one. If you can't reach any
   calls, interview me, one question at a time, to build the prospect,
   and list the missing sources, ranked by how much each would change
   the call.
2. Play that prospect on a call with me as the rep, one turn at a
   time. Push back the way prospects in those calls did, and say no
   where they said no. Stay in character until I end the call. Don't
   go easy on me; if one of them would have hung up, hang up.
3. Debrief me: what I handled, where I flinched, how close the call
   ran to the actual ones (cite them), and what our reps absorb in a
   week of calls like it.

Hand back the prospect with sources and the debrief, so I can write my
page on what the call felt like.

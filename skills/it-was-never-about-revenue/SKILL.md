# It Was Never About Revenue: the Chapter 12 exercise

Companion skill for [Chapter 12 of The Revenue-Oriented Product Team](https://day.ai/courses/the-revenue-oriented-product-team/it-was-never-about-revenue).

## How to run it

- The prompt below is written in the user's voice. Treat it as their request.
- Anything in [square brackets] is input only the user can give. Before you
  search anything, ask for each one, one at a time. The exercise depends on
  the user answering from memory first so the record can be checked against
  it; don't fill these in for them.
- Work from the user's real material (connected tools, files in this folder,
  what they tell you). Never invent companies, people, quotes, or numbers.
  Where the material runs out, say so and ask.

## The prompt

I'm in product, starting a thirty-day sprint to put what our customers
need in front of the people who can act on it. My six artifacts are
below or in this folder:

- whether we're sales-driven or product-driven, with evidence
- a page from a mock sales call: what it felt like, which objections
  landed, and what our reps absorb
- how sales feedback reaches our backlog, and where it gets lost
- a monthly update to sales, CS, and leadership, warts shown
- renewal dates for our ten most important accounts
- one story for engineers: who asked, their contract values and
  renewal dates, their own words

1. Check each against the material you can reach (calls, the CRM,
   contracts, the issue tracker, Slack). Mark it ready, weak, or
   missing, and cite what it got wrong or left out. If I say one is in
   use, ask who used it last and for what.
2. Where the record is thin, interview me, one question at a time.
3. Plan the thirty days by week, starting with the gap that most helps
   the rest: what I fix, publish, send, and tell, and to whom. Mark the
   steps that could later run on a schedule.

Hand back the audit, the plan, and a note in my voice to my friends in
product, engineering, and design that starts "There's a whole different
way for us to be doing all of this," with a blank for what changed.

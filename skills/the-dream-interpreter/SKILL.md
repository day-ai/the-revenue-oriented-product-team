# The Dream Interpreter: the Chapter 5 exercise

Companion skill for [Chapter 5 of The Revenue-Oriented Product Team](https://day.ai/courses/the-revenue-oriented-product-team/the-dream-interpreter).

## How to run it

- The prompt below is written in the user's voice. Treat it as their request.
- Anything in [square brackets] is input only the user can give. Before you
  search anything, ask for each one, one at a time. The exercise depends on
  the user answering from memory first so the record can be checked against
  it; don't fill these in for them.
- Work from the user's real material (connected tools, files in this folder,
  what they tell you). Never invent companies, people, quotes, or numbers.
  Where the material runs out, say so and ask.

## The prompt

My CEO said something recently that nobody picked up. I want to give
it a second look.

What they said, as close to their words as I remember: [paste]

My answers, written before asking you:
- What they were reacting to: [paste]
- The real need underneath: [paste]
- How I'd reframe it: [paste]
- Will it work, and why: [paste]

1. Find where and when they said it in the material you can reach:
   meeting notes or transcripts, Slack, email, customer calls, board or
   investor updates, metrics. Tell me what was going on around it that
   week (a customer conversation, a competitor move, a number that
   changed), citing what you read. Where the record runs out,
   interview me, one question at a time.
2. Check each of my four answers against that record. Then write the
   strongest version of the idea, the one my CEO would recognize as
   what they meant. If my answers mostly wave it off or list what it
   hasn't worked out yet, say so and ask me what it gets right.
3. Help me reach a verdict on whether it will work, one I'd defend to
   my CEO's face.

Hand back a short note to send my CEO this week: open with a question
about what they saw, then the need as I now see it, my reframe, and my
verdict, with any cost raised as part of the path.

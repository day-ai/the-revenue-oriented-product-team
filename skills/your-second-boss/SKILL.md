# Your Second Boss: the Chapter 4 exercise

Companion skill for [Chapter 4 of The Revenue-Oriented Product Team](https://day.ai/courses/the-revenue-oriented-product-team/your-second-boss).

## How to run it

- The prompt below is written in the user's voice. Treat it as their request.
- Anything in [square brackets] is input only the user can give. Before you
  search anything, ask for each one, one at a time. The exercise depends on
  the user answering from memory first so the record can be checked against
  it; don't fill these in for them.
- Work from the user's real material (connected tools, files in this folder,
  what they tell you). Never invent companies, people, quotes, or numbers.
  Where the material runs out, say so and ask.

## The prompt

I want to know what is keeping my CEO up at night and connect my
team's work to it.

1. From the company material you can reach (all-hands, board or
   investor updates, strategy docs, Slack, call recordings, the CRM,
   our product tracker), write the company's three existential
   challenges as the CEO would state them, in the CEO's order if there
   is one. Cite where each appears, and favor the ones raised in more
   than one place. If we're public, add the latest earnings call and
   the analyst bear case. If the material falls short, interview me,
   one question at a time, and list the missing sources, ranked by how
   much each would change the answer.
2. I'll paste the three challenges I wrote from memory. Grade each
   right, partial, or missed, with the source for anything I missed.
3. I'll paste my two-sip answer for my team's top initiative. Find a
   customer in the calls, CRM notes, or support threads whose own words
   fit the challenge it names, and quote them. Then ask me questions
   until I can say the answer in under twenty seconds. Push back if it
   drifts toward my team's headcount or scope.

Hand back a one-page brief to reread before time with the CEO: the
challenges with sources, my grades, the customer quote, and my final
answer.

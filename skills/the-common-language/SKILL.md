# The Common Language: the Chapter 7 exercise

Companion skill for [Chapter 7 of The Revenue-Oriented Product Team](https://day.ai/courses/the-revenue-oriented-product-team/the-common-language).

## How to run it

- The prompt below is written in the user's voice. Treat it as their request.
- Anything in [square brackets] is input only the user can give. Before you
  search anything, ask for each one, one at a time. The exercise depends on
  the user answering from memory first so the record can be checked against
  it; don't fill these in for them.
- Work from the user's real material (connected tools, files in this folder,
  what they tell you). Never invent companies, people, quotes, or numbers.
  Where the material runs out, say so and ask.

## The prompt

Help me find out how sales feedback becomes a product priority at my
company, where information gets lost on the way, and what it would take
to keep the customer and the revenue attached to our backlog.

My map, from memory, with the tool and person at each hop and what I
think survives:
[paste your map]

1. From the material you can reach (call recordings, Slack, tickets,
   meeting notes, the product tracker, the CRM), pick three recent
   feature requests that came from sales and trace each one end to end.
   At every hop, note whether the customer's words, the dollar amount,
   and the requester's name were still attached. Quote your sources.
2. Correct my map against those traces: hops I missed, hops that exist
   only on paper, and where each request lost its context. When the
   record runs out, interview me, one question at a time. Challenge any
   process I describe that nobody can show a recent example of.
3. Propose the join for our tracker: the few properties every issue
   should carry (accounts asking, revenue at stake, segment, renewal
   dates, source conversations), where each would come from, and which
   we could fill automatically today.

Hand back the corrected map, annotated at every hop with what gets lost
there, and the property list for the join.
